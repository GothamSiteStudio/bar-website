Residential Door Installations

Five Borough Door Solutions specializes in high-quality residential door installations. From modern entry systems to traditional wooden doors, our expert installers ensure strength, style, and long-term performance for every home in the five Borough.

Front & Entry Door Installations

Your front door is the first impression of your home. We install sturdy and energy-efficient doors built for security and elegance. Choose from steel, fiberglass, or solid wood doors — all professionally fitted and sealed for maximum durability and weather protection.

Interior & Bedroom Doors

Enhance your home’s design and privacy with our smooth-operating interior door installations. We install bedroom, closet, and bathroom doors that perfectly match your home’s aesthetic while improving insulation and noise reduction.

Sliding, Patio & Storm Doors

We handle sliding glass doors, patio doors, and weatherproof storm doors for homes across NYC. Whether you want to open your living space to natural light or add a protective barrier against harsh weather, our team ensures flawless alignment and easy operation.

Emergency Door Replacement

When damage or break-ins occur, you can rely on Five Borough Door Solutions for 24/7 emergency door replacement. Our mobile team arrives quickly, secures the area, removes damaged doors, and installs replacements on-site — keeping your family safe day or night.

Custom Fitting & Repair

Every home is unique — and so is every doorway. We provide custom fitting for non-standard frames, adjusting hinges, trimming, and weatherproofing for a seamless look. We also offer repair and reinforcement for warped, sticking, or misaligned doors.

High-Security & Smart Door Systems

Upgrade your home with high-security or smart door systems. From reinforced steel frames to keyless smart locks, our installation experts help protect your property while maintaining a sleek and modern appearance.
