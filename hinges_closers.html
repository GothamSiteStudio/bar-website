Continuous Hinge Installation

Continuous hinges support the full height of the door, spreading weight evenly and preventing frame damage and long-term sagging — especially on heavy or frequently used entrances.

  • Full-surface, half-surface & concealed continuous hinges
  • Great for aluminum storefront and hollow metal doors
  • Helps extend the lifespan of doors and frames

Door Closer Installation & Replacement

A properly adjusted door closer prevents slamming and ensures the door latches every time. We install and fine-tune closers for comfort and safety.

  • Surface, overhead concealed, and floor closers
  • Hold-open and non-hold-open configurations
  • ADA-friendly opening and closing force settings

Signs You Need New Hardware

Not sure if it’s time to replace your hinge or closer? These issues are common indicators.

  • Door slams, won’t latch, or is hard to open
  • Oil leaking from the door closer body
  • Door rubs on the floor or frame and drags
