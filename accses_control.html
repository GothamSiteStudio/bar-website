Access Control & Intercom Systems

Five Borough Door Solutions installs and integrates modern access control and intercom systems for residential and commercial buildings across New York City. From keypads and fobs to video intercoms and smart app access, we design secure, clean installations that work seamlessly with your aluminum, glass, and metal doors.

  • Apartments, lobbies & commercial spaces
  • Keypad, fob & card access systems
  • Audio & video intercom installations
  • Remote unlock & mobile app control
  • Integrated door, lock & maglock security

Access Control Solutions We Install

We design and install complete access control systems that manage who can enter your property and when. Whether you’re upgrading a single door or securing an entire building, we provide clean wiring, labeled panels, and hardware that’s built for heavy NYC usage.

  • Keypad / PIN code entry systems
  • Key fob and proximity card readers (RFID, Prox, etc.)
  • Magnetic locks and electric strikes integrated with door hardware
  • Smart lock access with app connectivity and remote unlock
  • Compatibility with alarms, cameras and automation platforms

Intercom Systems Installation

Intercom systems give tenants, staff, and residents a safe way to screen visitors before unlocking the door. We install both audio and video intercoms for everything from single-family homes to large multi-tenant buildings.

  • Multi-tenant video intercom panels for apartment and mixed-use buildings
  • Single-unit intercoms for private homes, offices, and storefronts
  • Door release integration for remote unlocking
  • Mobile app and touchscreen options for modern convenience
  • Replacement of outdated panels and wiring upgrades

Door Hardware & System Integration

A secure system is more than just readers and keypads — it has to work perfectly with your existing doors, frames, and hardware. We integrate access control with commercial-grade door components for smooth, code-compliant operation.

  • Maglocks, electric strikes, and RIM exit devices
  • Door closers, continuous hinges, and monitored door contacts
  • Request-to-exit (REX) sensors and push-to-exit buttons
  • Clean metal enclosures with labeled terminations and power supplies
  • Options for on-premise or cloud-based management

Why Upgrade to Access Control?

Moving from traditional keys to managed access gives you far more control over your building while reducing day-to-day headaches for owners and managers.

  • Eliminate constant rekeying after tenant turnover or lost keys
  • Control access by time, role, or location (cleaners, vendors, staff, tenants)
  • Review entry history and event logs when issues arise
  • Improve building security, curb appeal, and property value
  • Provide a more professional experience for residents and customers
