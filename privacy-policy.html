Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 26, 2026

1. Who We Are

Five Borough Door Solutions ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website fiveboroughdoorsolutions.com. We provide residential and commercial door installation, repair, and security hardware services across New York City, Westchester, and Long Island.

Contact us at: infofiveBoroughdoorsolutions@gmail.com or (929) 552-5516.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information you voluntarily provide when:

  • Submitting our quote or service request form — your name, phone number, email address, property address, and project details/photos.
  • Calling or emailing us directly — your contact details and service inquiry information.

We may also automatically collect basic non-personal technical data (such as browser type, device type, and pages visited) when you browse our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your door installation or repair quote request.
  • Schedule on-site measurements, consultations, and installations.
  • Provide customer support, warranty service, and invoicing.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Cookies and Third-Party Services

Our website may use basic functional cookies and third-party libraries (such as font and style CDNs) necessary to display our pages properly. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings.

5. Data Retention & Security

We retain service inquiries and customer records for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access or disclosure.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, please contact us at infofiveBoroughdoorsolutions@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
