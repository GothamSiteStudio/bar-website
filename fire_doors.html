Fire-Rated Metal Door Installations

Five Borough Door Solutions installs certified fire-rated metal doors for residential and commercial buildings across NYC. We focus on safety, code compliance, and long-term durability in stairwells, corridors, service rooms, and fire-exit paths.

  • UL fire-rated metal doors
  • Stairwells & exit corridors
  • Code-compliant hardware
  • Residential & commercial use
  • Clean installation & alignment

Where Fire Doors Are Used

Fire-rated doors are critical in high-occupancy and multi-unit buildings. We install them in key locations where fire separation and safe egress are required by code.

  • Stairwells and emergency exit corridors
  • Mechanical, boiler, electrical and utility rooms
  • Hallways in multi-family, office and commercial buildings

Fire-Rated Door Options

We supply and install hollow metal fire doors with the right rating for your application, along with matching frames and hardware.

  • 20, 45, 60 & 90-minute fire-rated assemblies (application-dependent)
  • Single and double door configurations
  • Optional vision lite glass with fire-rated glazing

Panic Bars & Exit Hardware

For safe and fast egress, we equip fire doors with panic hardware and closers that ensure the door latches properly after every use.

  • Panic exit devices and push bars
  • Fire-rated door closers and hinges
  • ADA-compliant lever sets and hardware options

Code-Conscious Installation

We install fire-rated doors with careful attention to alignment, closing force, latching, and frame anchoring, helping you meet safety requirements and inspection standards.

  • Proper swing direction and clearance
  • Verified self-closing and self-latching operation
  • Hardware and labeling appropriate to the rating
