Residential Door Installations

Five Borough Door Solutions provides professional aluminum, glass, and metal door installation across residential properties throughout New York City. We ensure strong security, clean workmanship, and long-lasting hardware — built to match your home or building perfectly.

  • Aluminum & Glass Entrance Doors
  • Lobby & Multi-Unit Doors
  • Hollow Metal Security Doors
  • Single & Double Door Configurations
  • Custom Fabrication & Hardware Upgrades

Aluminum & Glass Entrance Doors

Ideal for apartments, lobbies, and building entryways, these doors combine modern appearance with high durability. We provide precise measurement, fabrication, and installation to achieve a perfect fit and smooth operation.

  • Custom-sized fabrication for any opening
  • Compatible with intercoms, buzzers & access control
  • Available with continuous hinges, closers & cylinders

Residential Lobby & Multi-Unit Doors

We install secure and visually appealing entrance doors for residential buildings, brownstones, and multi-family properties. Built for heavy traffic and NYC weather conditions.

  • Strong commercial-grade frames & hardware
  • Ideal for co-ops, condos & rental buildings
  • Clean finishing and smooth-operation testing included

Hollow Metal Security Doors

For higher security needs, we install hollow metal doors with strong locking compatibility, perfect for exterior entrances, service areas, and secure residential applications.

  • 20–90 minute fire-rated options available
  • Compatible with panic hardware & mortise locks
  • Steel frames & reinforced hinges for long-term stability

Single & Double Door Installations

Whether upgrading a single entrance or installing double doors for larger openings, we handle exact alignment, hardware prep, and structural fitment to ensure flawless operation.

  • Interior and exterior configurations
  • Custom frame sizing & replacement
  • Precision alignment to prevent sagging or rubbing

Custom Sizing & Hardware Upgrades

Every residential property is unique — we customize hardware, hinges, locks, and door dimensions to meet your building requirements and security needs.

  • Continuous hinges & pivot hardware
  • Door closers, pull handles & mortise cylinders
  • Glass & frame customization options
